ShivamModicalClinic Mobile App is a modern, motion-rich medical practice & patient portal that makes appointment scheduling, online/offline visits, labs and tests integration, prescription, follow-up, notification, reminders, AI augmentation feature that can predict or inform future risk or problem may arise due to current condition and vitals of a patient, clinical records, and patient follow-up delightful for doctors and simple for patients. Built for mobile-first (React Native + Acentry UI + Express + MongoDB).
